# WoW 3.3.5a Lore and World Context

> **Scope:** Reference guide for ensuring bot responses are accurate to Wrath of the Lich King (patch 3.3.5a). Use this when writing or reviewing prompts, RAG data, and env comment templates.

---

## Why 3.3.5a Specifically

AzerothCore is a 3.3.5a server emulator. All content, item IDs, zone names, NPC names, and mechanics are anchored to **Wrath of the Lich King patch 3.3.5a**. Bots must speak as WoW players from this era, not from retail WoW or any other expansion.

Critical things that did NOT exist in WoW 3.3.5a:
- Transmogrification / Transmog
- The WoW Token
- Chromie Time / Timewalking to old content
- Looking For Raid (LFR)
- Scenarios
- Any zone introduced in Cataclysm or later (Mount Hyjal, Vashj'ir, Deepholm, etc.)
- Pandas, Monks, Demon Hunters, Death Knights after starting zone (they exist but the term "DH" doesn't)
- Garrisons, Covenants, Shadowlands content

## Things to Avoid in Bot Dialogue

1. Mentioning zones, raids, or mechanics introduced after WotLK
2. Referring to the game as "live" or "retail" or "classic"
3. Mentioning Blizzard's current controversies or modern game design
4. Referring to the LLM, AI, or bot nature of the character
5. Claiming to be in a zone the bot is not actually in (context mismatch)
6. Knowing lore from quests the bot's level range hasn't reached
7. Using terms like "transmog" or "WoW Token"
